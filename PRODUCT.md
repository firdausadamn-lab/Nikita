# PRODUCT · The Wrestler's Home

**Client:** CRAFT  
**Athlete partner:** Nikita  
**Register:** brand for public marketing, product for the member training environment.

## Product

A bilingual, mobile-first Greco-Roman strength and conditioning platform. The initial offer is a one-time eight-week digital course. The architecture is ready for verified checkout, authenticated entitlement, coaching applications, multiple programs, and community later.

## Audience

Beginner and intermediate athletes, former wrestlers, combat-sport participants, and people who want functional strength, explosiveness, grip, neck, trunk, and conditioning without a bodybuilding-only focus.

## Emotional idea

The quiet before training. Repetition becoming instinct. The mat is demanding, familiar, and welcoming. The product is a place to return to when the athlete needs structure.

## Voice

Direct, calm, experienced, disciplined, and internationally understandable. No shame, fake urgency, invented credentials, aggressive masculinity, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Brand direction

Working name: **The Wrestler's Home / Дом борца**  
Working line: **Strength has a home. / Сила обрела дом.**

## Current implementation boundary

The complete interface and development adapters are included. Production authentication, payment, webhook entitlement, private media, email, and persistent account data require provider credentials and final client decisions.

## Anti-references

Generic fitness landing pages, SaaS hero-metric templates, Russian-flag sports clichés, glossy wellness brands, trophy-wall credibility, fake scarcity, and hyper-aggressive training language.
